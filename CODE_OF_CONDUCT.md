# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can ask a question, report a bug
or send a fix without being belittled for it. We pledge to make participation a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the tools.
- Assume good faith, and say plainly what is wrong rather than who is wrong.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes, and say so when something you reported turns out to be your
  own setup.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or credentials,
  without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, comments and any other space where someone
is representing the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may
edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not
follow them, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour
they judge inappropriate.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@sazzadursiam](https://github.com/sazzadursiam),
through the private report form under **Security → Report a vulnerability**. Say
in the report that it is a conduct issue. Reports are handled privately, and the
person reported is not told who made it.
